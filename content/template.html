Tips for Posting Multiple Categories:
Audience Clarity:
Thoda time lagta hai audience ko samajhne mein ki tumhara account kis type ka content deta hai. Agar categories mix kar rahe ho, to ek pattern banao (e.g. Monday – motivation, Wednesday – tech, Friday – memes).

Profile Optimization:
Bio mein likho ki tum kya-kya share karte ho (e.g. "Tech | Motivation | Daily Facts").

Hashtags ka Use:
Har category ke relevant hashtags use karo taaki woh right audience tak pahuche.

Consistency:
Har category ka content barabar frequency se post karo, taaki kisi bhi interest group ka engagement kam na ho.

Thread Format:
Educational ya tech content thread mein do, jabki memes aur opinions short tweets ho sakte hain.
